# matezoo

* `fishmates_v1.epd.gz`: A collection of 1M mates from LTC fishtest games, ranging from #3 to #35. The positions have between 6 and 31 pieces, and in 202213 positions the side to move is going to get mated.
See [plot](images/fishmates_v1.png?raw=true). 
(This collection was moved here from its origin at
[robertnurnberg/matetools](https://github.com/robertnurnberg/matetools)
in August 2026.)
